## 1. Who we are · 关于我们

Camping Nigeria Limited rents camping equipment and runs outdoor events in Abuja, Nigeria. Our customers are schools, companies and private guests. Each tent we rent is used many times a year, outdoors, in strong sun, heat, humidity and heavy rain.

Camping Nigeria Limited 是尼日利亚阿布贾的一家露营装备租赁和户外活动公司。我们的客户包括学校、企业和个人。我们出租的每一顶帐篷每年会被使用很多次，长期在户外，面对强烈日晒、高温、潮湿和大雨。

We are building **complete premium sleeping kits**: a tent and everything a guest needs to sleep well, packed in one bag. This order is our first premium stock.

我们正在打造**完整的高端睡眠套装**：一顶帐篷加上客人睡好觉所需的全部物品，装在一个包里。这是我们的第一批高端货品。

## 2. What we care about most · 我们最重视的事

**1. Premium, export-grade quality.** We want the same quality you would sell to buyers in Europe, the USA or Australia. **Do not offer us a cheaper "Africa grade" or "Nigeria market" version.** We will not accept factory seconds, B-grade stock, old stock, or lighter fabric than the sample.

**1. 高端出口级品质。** 我们要的是与您卖给欧洲、美国或澳大利亚客户相同的品质。**请不要给我们提供便宜的"非洲版"或"尼日利亚市场版"低配产品。** 我们不接受次品、B级品、库存旧货，也不接受比样品更薄更轻的面料。

**2. The approved sample is the standard.** Bulk goods must match the approved sample in fabric, weight, poles, zips, stitching, colour and packing. If anything changes, ask us in writing **before** production.

**2. 确认样（封样）就是标准。** 大货必须在面料、克重、帐篷杆、拉链、缝制、颜色和包装上与确认样完全一致。任何改动都必须在生产**之前**以书面形式征得我们同意。

**3. Products that last in heat, sun and rain.** We rent each item many times. Durability matters more than the lowest price.

**3. 耐高温、耐晒、耐雨。** 每件产品我们都会多次出租。耐用性比最低价格更重要。

**4. Complete kits.** We would rather buy 25 complete kits than 50 tents with missing parts. Our sourcing agent should cover every item on the list across the selected suppliers. Each supplier should quote the items they can supply and clearly mark anything they cannot supply.

**4. 完整套装。** 我们宁愿买25套完整的套装，也不要50顶缺配件的帐篷。采购代理应通过选定的供应商覆盖清单上的每一项。每家供应商请对能供应的产品报价，并明确标注不能供应的产品。

**5. Honest answers.** If a product cannot meet a spec, tell us. We would rather change the plan than receive the wrong product.

**5. 诚实沟通。** 如果某个产品达不到规格，请直接告诉我们。我们宁愿调整计划，也不想收到不合格的产品。

## 3. Material rules for all products · 所有产品的材料要求

- **Polyester, not nylon,** for anything that stays in the sun.
  长期日晒的产品用**涤纶（聚酯纤维），不要尼龙**。
- **Hydrolysis-resistant coating** (polyether PU or TPU), so the coating does not go sticky or flake in heat and humidity.
  **抗水解涂层**（聚醚型PU或TPU），在高温潮湿环境下涂层不发粘、不脱落。
- **Anti-mildew treatment** on all fabric.
  所有面料做**防霉处理**。
- **UV-stabilised** fabric and webbing (UPF 50+ on tent flies and canopies).
  面料和织带需**抗紫外线**（帐篷外帐和天幕 UPF 50+）。
- **No fibreglass poles.** Aluminium alloy or steel only.
  **不要玻璃纤维杆。** 只接受铝合金杆或钢杆。
- **No built-in lithium batteries**, except in the solar fan + light combos (see section 7). Please confirm this for every item, including pumps and string lights.
  **不要内置锂电池**，太阳能风扇灯除外（见第7节）。每一件产品都请确认这一点，包括打气泵和串灯。
- **Colour: light earth tones** (sand, beige, light khaki) for tents and tarps. Dark colours make tents hotter in the sun. Dark green trim is fine.
  **颜色：浅色大地色系**（沙色、米色、浅卡其色），用于帐篷和防水布。深色在阳光下会让帐篷更热。深绿色的边饰可以接受。
- **Mains electricity in Nigeria:** 220–240 V, 50 Hz, UK 3-pin plug (Type G).
  **尼日利亚电压：** 220–240伏，50赫兹，英标三脚插头（G型）。
