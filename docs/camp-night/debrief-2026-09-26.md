# Camp Night debrief — 26 September 2026

Rough notes from the night: guest feedback, ideas, and people to follow up with.
Sorted by theme; nothing here is decided yet.

## 1. Tents and sleeping kit

- **Mattresses got wet — waterproofing is the top fix.**
  - Thicker, more waterproof tents (also easier for us to maintain)
  - Tarp under each tent (floor)
  - Tarp over the top of each tent
- **Mattresses:** twin inflatable ones — thin, quick to inflate, stackable, easy to move around
- **Comfort inside the tent:**
  - Lights that can hang inside
  - Small battery-powered hanging fans to keep tents cool
- **Brand every tent** with the CN logo
- **Employ a cleaner** for the tents

## 2. Site operations

- **Trash cans: 1 per 7 tents** (≈ 9 cans at the 60-tent cap). Ratio still to be sanity-checked.
- **Drawstring bags** for guests, to make clean-up easier
- **More speakers**, spread around the site so the sound is dispersed rather than concentrated in one spot
- **Gate charge to drive in** — guests were charged at the gate. To decide: build it into the ticket price, or warn guests before they arrive. (Open: who charged it?)

## 3. Tracking

- **Track sign-ups (barcode).** Every sign-up already gets an `SCN-XXXXXX` code from `/api/camp-night-signup`. Rendering it as a QR code on the confirmation email and page would give us scan-in at the gate — small website change.
- **Track the tents themselves.** Numbered tag on each tent + a "Tent ID" column in the Camp Night sheet, filled in at check-in. Maps each `SCN` code to a tent, which also helps with cleaning and damage checks.

## 4. Food and drink

- Noodles were too soft — raise food quality
- Include suya (or similar) in the price
- Provide drinks (possibly bottles for some guests)
- Guests would prefer multiple vendors over one

## 5. Packages and pricing

- **Tables and chairs** as a paid add-on
- **VIP section** — requested by Daniel
- **Extra charge for pets**
- **Rename the "Bush" packaging** to something more palatable (not used anywhere on the website)
