# Camping Nigeria Focus Plan: 14 October to 31 December 2026

**Team:** Wole, Taiye, Kehinde, Toyosi
**Drawn from:** [Growth and Market Opportunity Research](../../Camping_Nigeria_Growth_Market_Opportunity_Research.md) (6 September 2026). Section numbers below (§) point into that document.
**Reviewed:** monthly, at the start of November, December and January. See [Monthly review](#monthly-review).

## The goal for the rest of 2026

The research sets out five phases. Only the first one belongs in this year:

> **Become the best camping rental company in Nigeria. Make renting easy, reliable and professional.** (§75)

The China trip adds one more aim: come back with suppliers we trust, and decide what we will buy from them.

### What should be true by 31 December 2026

1. **We know exactly what we own.** Every rental asset has an ID, a recorded cost and a condition. No gear goes back out without being inspected.
2. **We rent kits, not loose items.** Family, School Expedition, Organization Camp and Field Team kits have fixed contents and prices, and they are on the website.
3. **We know which suppliers we trust.** Every factory is scored, samples have been tested in Abuja, landed costs are worked out, and a first order is either placed or deliberately postponed.
4. **We know what customers need.** We have spoken to 10 schools and 10 organizations about the equipment they rent or buy, and we have bookings for the January–March dry season.
5. **We look after our gear.** A cleaning and repair routine is written down and in use, and we hold a starting stock of spare parts.
6. **One register holds all of it.** An internal asset register records the above. We are not building a software product yet (§57).
7. **Someone owns gear care full-time.** Three Gear Care Technicians have accepted offers and start on 4 January 2027, trained on the cleaning and repair routine from outcome 5.

## What we are not doing this year

These come from the research's own warnings (§18, §20, §57, §65, §70):

- No retail store or showroom.
- No private-label tents. Private label waits for rental data, and starts with accessories.
- No footwear stock and no broad apparel range.
- No Lagos stock point.
- No UN or humanitarian tenders.
- No software for sale. Software this year only runs our own operation.
- No fuel canisters and no climbing gear.
- No product without a job. Every new item must answer the six questions in §65 before we buy it in volume.

## The sequence

### Stage 1: China trip (from 14 October)

**Aim: decide which suppliers to work with. Buy samples, not stock.**

- Tag every factory meeting with its purpose: rent, sell, private-label or distribute (§62).
- Score every factory out of 100 on the factory scorecard (§63).
- Order samples of 15–20 priority products from the Tier 1 list (§61). Use the [gear sourcing guide](../../Camping%20Nigeria%20Gear%20Sourcing.md) for specs and factory questions.
- At home, the inventory count and sales conversations start without waiting for the trip to end.

### Stage 2: November (test and decide)

- Samples arrive and are tested in real use.
- Work out the landed cost of each priority product: factory price, freight, duty, SONCAP, clearing, and transport to Abuja.
- **Decide on the first order by 20 November.**
- The inventory baseline is finished, and kit contents are fixed.
- Customer interviews are done, and proposals for the dry season are out.

### Stage 3: December (launch and lock in)

- Kits and a clearer rental catalogue go live on the website.
- If the decision was yes, the deposit on the first order is paid.
- January–March bookings are confirmed.
- Year-end review, then the plan for January–March 2027.

### Timing to check before ordering

These are rough estimates; confirm them with the factory and the freight forwarder.

- Production takes 30–60 days after the sample is approved. Sea freight to Lagos takes about 4–6 weeks, then clearing and the road to Abuja.
- **Chinese New Year falls on 6 February 2027.** Factories close for several weeks around it, so an order that isn't finished by mid-January may not ship until March.
- That puts an order placed in late November in Abuja around March 2027, near the end of the dry season. This is why the decision date is 20 November and not the end of the month.
- The January–March season will therefore run mostly on the gear we already own. If new stock has to arrive before then, Wole and Taiye need to decide during the trip.

> **Assumption:** Wole and Taiye travel, and Kehinde and Toyosi are in Abuja. If that's wrong, swap the October items between people.

## Monthly review

**When:** the first working days of November, December and January. When a Claude Code session opens in this repo, a hook checks whether this month's review file exists in [reviews/](reviews/). If it doesn't, Claude raises it at the start of the session.

**How it works:**

1. Each owner gives the status of their outcomes for the checkpoint that just passed.
2. Update the **Status board** above.
3. For anything **Behind**, record why, then either set a new date or drop it.
4. Write each person's steps for the coming month: the next column of their table, adjusted for what slipped.
5. List any decisions the team needs to make.
6. Save it as `reviews/YYYY-MM.md`, named for the month the review happens in (the November review is `reviews/2026-11.md`), and share it with the team.

**The January review is the year-end review.** It scores the seven year-end outcomes at the top of this plan, then sets the January–March 2027 plan. At that point, point the hook at the new plan file (the path is at the top of [.claude/hooks/focus-review-check.mjs](../../.claude/hooks/focus-review-check.mjs)).
