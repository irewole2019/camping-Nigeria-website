## Why we're hiring

The research's whole rental model depends on gear being inspected, cleaned, dried and repaired between every booking (§24, §25, §45, §46). Until now that work has fallen on the founders. Three full-time technicians give us:

- cover for leave, sickness and busy post-event days;
- a team that can run the gear states and repair log the plan sets up;
- the repair and cleaning capability that the research later expects to sell as a service to schools, churches and other operators (§24, §25).

**Order matters.** Toyosi writes the cleaning routine, kit contents lists and repair log *before* the hires start (see the focus plan, November column). Those documents become the training material. If they slip, the technicians start without a system.

## Screening rubric

Score each application before the screening calls.

**Must-have** (fails any → do not shortlist)

1. At least 6 months' work in sewing or tailoring, upholstery, laundry or dry-cleaning, or equipment maintenance.
2. Lives in Abuja or can commute daily.
3. Has given a working phone number.

**Should-have** (each scored 0–3, then weighted; maximum 100)

| Criterion | Weight | 0 | 3 |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Relevant hands-on experience | 35 | Under a year, unrelated | 2+ years in sewing, repair or laundry |
| Experience with heavy fabrics or outdoor gear (tents, tarps, canvas, bags, upholstery) | 25 | None mentioned | Clear, specific examples or photos |
| Stability: time in previous roles | 20 | Several jobs under 3 months | A year or more in at least one role |
| Clear, complete application | 20 | Missing details, hard to follow | Clear, complete, followed the instructions (subject line, details) |

Score = sum of (score ÷ 3 × weight). Invite **60 and above** to a screening call, to a maximum of 10 calls.

**Nice-to-have** (breaks ties only): industrial sewing machine, event rental work, camping experience, photos of their own work.

## Interview process

| Stage | Who | Length | What it assesses |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Screening call | Toyosi | 15–20 minutes | Logistics, availability, basic experience, communication |
| 2. Working interview | Toyosi, plus Wole or Taiye | About 2 hours, at the store | Attention to detail, repair skill, care and routine, recording |
| 3. Terms talk | Toyosi | 15 minutes | Pay, hours, start date, references or guarantor |

Each competency is scored at one stage only, so the two interviewers aren't marking the same thing twice.

### Stage 1: Screening call (Toyosi)

**Assesses:** reliability and logistics, and communication.

Questions:

1. Tell me about the work you do now, or did most recently. What does a normal day look like?
2. What's the most difficult repair or cleaning job you've done? What did you do, step by step?
3. This job includes some weekends and event days, with time off in the week. How would that work for you?
4. How would you get to our base each day, and how long would it take?
5. Tell me about a time you noticed a problem at work that nobody else had spotted. What did you do?

Probes: *What happened in the end? Who did you tell? What would you do differently?*

**Reliability and logistics**
- **5:** Clear, workable travel plan. Comfortable with weekend work. Long, steady previous roles they explain well.
- **3:** Workable plan with small doubts, such as a long commute or limited weekend availability.
- **1:** Can't commit to weekend or event days, or has no realistic way to get to work.

**Communication**
- **5:** Answers are specific and easy to follow, and they ask sensible questions about the job.
- **3:** Gets the point across with some prompting.
- **1:** Answers are vague, or they cannot describe their own work.

### Stage 2: Working interview (Toyosi, plus Wole or Taiye)

Prepare **one used tent with planted faults** before the session:
- a small tear in the fly;
- a worn or loose zip slider;
- a cracked or bent pole section;
- a damp patch or spot of mould;
- a peg and a guy line missing from the bag.

Write the list of planted faults down in advance. Give the candidate the kit contents list and a phone with the repair-log form.

| Task | Time | Lead interviewer | Competency |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Inspect the tent and list every fault you find | 30 minutes | Toyosi | Attention to detail |
| B. Fix two of them: replace the zip slider and patch the tear (or re-thread a shock cord) | 45 minutes | Second interviewer | Repair skill |
| C. Say how you would clean and dry this tent before it goes back on the shelf, then pack it to the contents list | 20 minutes | Toyosi | Care and routine |
| D. Log what you found and fixed on the phone form | 10 minutes | Second interviewer | Recording |
| Short conversation (questions below) | 15 minutes | Both | (adds evidence to the above) |

Questions during the conversation:

1. You found five problems. Which would you fix first, and why?
2. A tent comes back soaking wet late on a Sunday. What do you do with it?
3. Tell me about a time you had to redo work because it wasn't good enough. What happened?
4. Something comes back damaged and you can't fix it. Who do you tell, and what do you record?

Probes: *Show me. Why that way? What would happen if you skipped that step?*

**Attention to detail** (task A)
- **5:** Finds all five planted faults, plus anything real we missed, and checks methodically: unpacks fully, checks seams and zips end to end.
- **3:** Finds three or four, including the obvious tear and the missing parts.
- **1:** Finds two or fewer, or never unpacks the bag fully.

**Repair skill** (task B)
- **5:** Both repairs are neat, strong and done the right way. They explain why: for example, a new slider instead of forcing the zip, and a patch sealed on both sides.
- **3:** Both repairs work but are rough, or one is good and one needs help.
- **1:** Can't complete either repair, or the repairs fail when tested.

**Care and routine** (task C)
- **5:** Insists on drying fully before packing. Treats or flags the mould. Kit is packed complete to the list and labelled.
- **3:** Right idea with small gaps, such as forgetting to check the contents list.
- **1:** Would pack the tent damp, or packs it incomplete.

**Recording** (task D)
- **5:** Every fault and repair logged clearly, with a photo where useful.
- **3:** Main points logged but incomplete.
- **1:** Can't or won't use the form.

### Stage 3: Terms talk (Toyosi)

Not scored. Confirm pay, hours, weekend arrangements, probation, start date, and references or guarantor. Answer the candidate's questions.

### Debrief

1. Each interviewer writes down their scores **before** discussing, so nobody anchors on anyone else.
2. Spend the discussion on scores that differ by two or more.
3. Treat these separately from the score. Any one of them rules a candidate out, however well they scored:
   - would pack gear damp or incomplete;
   - dishonest about their experience;
   - can't commit to weekend or event days.
4. Ask: *"Would we trust this person to prepare the tents for a school expedition, unsupervised?"*
5. Hire the three strongest who clear the bar. If fewer than three do, hire those who do and re-advertise. Don't fill a seat with someone below the bar.
