# Gear Care Technician (3 roles, full-time, Abuja)

Every tent, sleeping bag and lantern that goes out from Camping Nigeria comes back to be checked, cleaned, dried and repaired before the next family, school or company uses it. We're hiring three people to own that work, and to make sure nothing damaged, dirty or incomplete ever goes out again.

## About Camping Nigeria

Camping Nigeria runs outdoor programmes for schools, organizations and individuals, and rents camping gear across Abuja. Children sleep in our tents on school expeditions, and companies use our kit for retreats. The quality of our gear is the quality of our name. We're now growing our rental fleet and building a proper workshop to look after it.

## About the role

You'll be part of a small team that keeps our equipment in top condition. A year from now, success looks like this:

- Every item in the fleet has a clean record.
- Every kit leaves the store complete.
- Wet or damaged returns are handled the same day.
- Our gear lasts years longer because it is cared for properly.

You'll report to Toyosi, who leads operations, maintenance and events.

## What you'll do

- **Inspect every return.** Check each tent, mat, sleeping bag, chair and light against its checklist. Find the tear, the worn zip slider, the cracked pole section and the missing peg before a customer does.
- **Clean, dry and re-proof gear.** Wash sleeping bags and liners, and fully dry tents so they never go into storage damp. Treat fabric against mould and re-waterproof it when needed.
- **Repair what can be repaired.** This includes patching fabric, replacing zip sliders and shock cord, swapping pole sections, re-stitching seams and straps, and replacing chair fabric.
- **Pack kits to the list.** Make up Family, School and Organization kits so that every component is present and every bag is labelled.
- **Keep the records.** Scan each item's QR code with a phone as it moves from returned, to cleaning, to drying, to repair, to ready. Log every repair: what was wrong and what you did.
- **Keep the store in order.** Keep returned, drying, repair and ready gear in their own areas, and count spare parts.
- **Support camps and events.** Help load, set up and pack down on event days, some of which fall on weekends.

## What's hard about this job

It is physical, hands-on work, and sometimes dirty: muddy tents after a rainy weekend, mouldy fabric, heavy loads. The busiest days follow weekends and events. Attention to detail matters more than speed, because one missed fault can spoil a child's first night camping.

## What we're looking for

**Required**

- At least 6 months' work in sewing or tailoring, upholstery, laundry or dry-cleaning, or equipment maintenance.
- Able to use a smartphone for WhatsApp, taking photos and scanning codes.
- Able to read and write English well enough to follow a checklist and fill in a repair log.
- Able to lift and carry loads of about 20 kg and work on your feet.
- Available to work some weekends and event days, with time off arranged in the week.
- Living in Abuja, or able to travel to our base in Abuja every working day.

**Nice to have**

- Experience on an industrial or heavy-duty sewing machine.
- Experience repairing tents, tarpaulins, canvas, bags or outdoor gear.
- Experience with event rentals or equipment hire.
- Camping or outdoor experience of any kind.

You don't need a degree or a certificate. We'll train you on our gear, our routines and our system.
