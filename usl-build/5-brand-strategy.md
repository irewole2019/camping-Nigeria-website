# PART 1: BRAND FOUNDATION ONE-PAGE

## Why this exists
The MAD Settings page promises guests "a closed guest list". Today the gate cannot keep that promise: codes are glanced at, not checked. Padi Check makes the promise true without making guests feel suspected.

## Who it serves
- **Guests** (email, confirmation page): want to get in fast with no fuss. Tone: warm, welcoming.
- **The volunteer at the gate** (scanner, gate card): wants to know who to let in and to know a founder has their back. Tone: plain, calm, instructive.
- **Founders** own the tool and decide on every red; they are not an audience for the copy.

Audience architecture: separate expressions under one voice (guest-facing warm; volunteer-facing plain).

## Position it owns
Against the glance check, a printed list, a Sheets add-on or a ticketing platform: the only option built on Camping Nigeria's own codes and emails, where a person (Toyosi) always handles red. Internal positioning only; nothing is sold.

## Brand Idea
**"You are in."** Already the headline of the confirmation email. Every word Padi Check shows should lead a paid guest to that moment.

## How it shows up
- **Voice:** short sentences, second person, warm to guests, calm to the volunteer. Matches "Keep this. It is how we find you at the gate."
- **Visual:** existing brand tokens (forest green `#0e3e2e`, gold `#e6b325`, cream `#f3efe6`); result screens use full-screen colour (green, orange, red) with large type, readable outdoors at night.
- **Behaviour:** when something goes wrong, Padi Check never accuses the guest. Red points to a person, not a verdict.

## Named expressions

| Where | Words |
|---|---|
| Tool name (internal, scanner header) | **Padi Check** |
| Guest-facing name for the QR | **Gate pass** |
| Back-fill email subject | Your gate pass for MAD Settings |
| Instruction under the QR | Screenshot this now. Show it at the gate. |
| Green screen | **Welcome in** / [Name] / [Ticket] · Tent or No tent |
| Orange screen | **Already checked in** / [Name] / at [time] |
| Red, not found | **We cannot find this code** / [Call Toyosi] / Try typing it |
| Red, not paid | **Not marked as paid** / [Call Toyosi] |
| Network failure | Cannot reach the list. Try again. |
| Printed volunteer card | Gate card |

## Guardrails
- Never say: fraud-proof, secure, INVALID, DENIED, or any claim of fraud prevented.
- Never shout in capitals on red. Never show a guest's phone or email on the scanner.
- Red always offers a person (Call Toyosi), never "deny entry".

# PART 2: DETAILED STRATEGY AND TRANSFER PACKET

## DISCOVERY SUMMARY
**Core belief:** Guests who paid should walk in welcomed; the "closed guest list" promise should be real.
**Gap or tension:** The gate checks nothing, so the promise is unenforced; checking harder risks making guests feel suspected.
**Founding conviction:** Check properly, welcome warmly.

## BRAND DEFINITION
**Name:** Padi Check ("padi" is Nigerian Pidgin for friend: a friend at the gate checking you in). User-chosen 2026-10-17.
**Category:** Internal event check-in tool.
**Geography and languages:** Abuja, Nigeria; English with Pidgin in the name only.
**Architecture:** Internal tool under the Camping Nigeria masterbrand; guest-facing surfaces carry Camping Nigeria and "gate pass", not the tool name.

## TARGET AUDIENCE
**User:** Volunteer gate operative (avoidance register: wants to avoid blame and long lines).
**Secondary:** Guests (want fast, no-fuss entry).
**Payer Decision Profile:** Founders; internal; no external payer. Trigger: MAD Settings on 31 Oct 2026. Proof required: rehearsal pass on 28 Oct.
**Audience architecture:** Separate expressions under one voice.

## POSITIONING
**Positioning statement:** For Camping Nigeria's gate volunteers, Padi Check is the event check-in tool that turns every code into a clear, calm answer, because it checks the live guest list behind the scenes and hands every red to a named person.
**Alternatives:** Glance check; printed list; Sheets QR add-on; ticketing platform scanner (Stage 1 research).
**Map:** Not plotted; internal tool with no competitive positioning to defend.

## MARKET EVIDENCE AND SOURCES
No live research run this session. Stage 1 sources reused.

## BRAND HOUSE (compressed)
**Purpose:** This tool exists to make "a closed guest list" true while every paid guest still feels welcome.
**Audience insight:** The volunteer fears blame more than slowness. [reasoned]
**Market insight:** Not applicable (internal).
**Point of difference:** Own codes and emails; a person on every red.
**Brand promise:** No paid guest is turned away because of the tech.
**Values:** Welcoming, clear, fair.
**Personality:** Warm, calm, plain-spoken, dependable, friendly.

## BRAND LADDER
- **Attributes:** QR gate pass; live list check; full-screen colours; typed fallback; Call Toyosi.
- **Functional benefits:** Instant yes or no; reused codes caught; arrivals recorded.
- **User benefits:** Volunteer is sure and backed up; guest gets in fast.
- **Emotional end benefit:** Belonging from the first step through the gate.
**Brand Idea:** You are in.

## MANIFESTO
If you paid, you belong here. You should not have to argue at the gate. A guest list only means something if someone checks it. Checking should feel like being welcomed, not questioned. When something does not match, a person sorts it out, not a machine. Everyone through the gate should hear the same thing: you are in.

## BRAND IDENTITY SYSTEM
**Practical constraints:** Phone screens outdoors at night; one-handed use; Android and iPhone Safari; existing brand tokens and fonts (Agrandir, DM Sans, local files); colour must not be the only signal (each result also has a large word for colour-blind volunteers).
**Chosen route:** Existing Camping Nigeria system. Scanner chrome in forest green with the Padi Check name; results as full-screen green, orange and red with large DM Sans text and the result word in Agrandir.
**Routes considered:** A separate playful Padi Check identity (rejected: splits the brand for an internal tool).
**Brand behaviour:** (1) Never accuse; red points to a person. (2) Always offer a way through (type the code, call Toyosi). (3) Say less on screen; one big word, then details.

## BRAND NAME DECISION
**Tool name:** Padi Check. Internal, not published or sold. Screening status: Unverified (no collision or trademark check run); acceptable for internal use. Re-screen before any external use or sale.
