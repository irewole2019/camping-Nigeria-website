## THE OFFER IN ONE PARAGRAPH

An internal check-in tool, not a product for sale. Every guest at a Camping Nigeria paid event gets a personal QR in their confirmation email and on their confirmation page. A volunteer at the gate opens a password-protected scanner on their phone, scans or types the code, and sees a full-screen green (name, ticket, tent), orange (already checked in at a time) or red (not found, or not marked paid, with a Call founder button). Green records the arrival in the event's Google Sheet. It costs the founders 3 to 5 build days and NGN 0, and its commitment to guests is that no paid guest is turned away because of the tech.

---

## WHO THIS IS FOR

**Niche:** The volunteer gate operative at a Camping Nigeria paid, gated event, starting with MAD Settings on 31 Oct 2026; secondarily, the guests arriving at that gate.

**Their central tension (central motive), in one sentence:**
> "Tell me quickly and clearly who to let in, so I am not the one blamed for a mistake or a long line." [reasoned]

**Prior behavior:** At Camp Night, officials glanced at the code format only; nothing went wrong, and nothing could have been detected.

**Explicitly not for:** Non-gated or free events; school programs; founders doing ad hoc lookups (the sheet serves them).
